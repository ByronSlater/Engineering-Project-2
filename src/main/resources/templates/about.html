About Acebook

Welcome to Acebook, a place to connect, share, and keep up with the people who matter to you! Whether you're posting an update or catching up with friends, Acebook gives you a simple space to stay social.

Acebook is a full-stack social networking app built with Java, Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, and PostgreSQL, with Auth0 and Spring Security keeping accounts safe. Testing and database migrations help keep it reliable as it grows.
